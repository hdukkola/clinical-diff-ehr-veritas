## 5. Review of `docs/WIDGET_BUILD_TEMPLATE.md` & First Widget Recommendation

### Template Assessment ([WIDGET_BUILD_TEMPLATE.md](WIDGET_BUILD_TEMPLATE.md))
The shared template is crisp and covers the real failure modes in Clinical Diff (`Morgan Lee (Synthetic)` / `DEMO-001` stability, explicit `Source unavailable` vs. documented absence, stationary facts during reading, 390/768/desktop checks, keyboard & touch access, reduced motion, and independent review). Two lightweight refinements worth considering in future PRs:
1. Add an explicit **Synthetic & Non-PHI Gate** line under *Acceptance* (confirming no real patient IDs, secrets, or unverified live-model outputs appear in the widget).
2. Clarify **Focus Return** explicitly under *Interaction* (when closing a drill-down drawer/popover, focus must return to the invoking trigger element).

### Recommended First Widget to Build: **Backlog #01 — Source Evidence Drill-Down (`provenance-health`)**
**Recommendation:** Build **#01 Source evidence drill-down** first, exactly as seeded in `WIDGET_BUILD_TEMPLATE.md`.

**Reasoning:**
1. **Closes the highest-trust gap first:** Across Epic, Oracle Health, Practice Fusion, Greenway Intergy, and patient portals (`MyChart` / `athenaPatient`), the defining safety property of clinical reconciliation is one-click verification of *where* a claim came from (`source_id`, timestamp, and literal quote) — especially when distinguishing patient-reported edits from clinical notes or missing documentation.
2. **Maximum reuse, minimal blast radius:** `provenance-health` already exists inside `CockpitWidgets`, and `analyzeSnapshots` in `src/lib/diff.ts` already resolves `EvidenceLink` spans, `missing_source`, and `ambiguous` flags. Item #01 wires the UI drill-down directly to existing typed contracts without inventing new synthetic datasets or backend endpoints.
3. **Unblocks downstream backlog items (#02–#03, #11–#17):** Evidence state labels (#02), Note navigator (#03), and Medication reconciliation details (#11–#17) all rely on a clean, keyboard/touch-accessible excerpt drill-down pattern with verified focus return.
